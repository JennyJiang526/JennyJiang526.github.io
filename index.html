Benchmarking AI Agents for Addressing Multi-Scale Scientific Challenges
Research Assistant · Prof. Hongyu Zhao, Yale University

Built reproducible analysis and visualization pipelines for large-scale comparative model evaluation.
03/2026 – Present
End-to-End Nextflow Data Processing Pipeline for Expanding the VDJbase Database
Research Assistant · Prof. Gur Yaari, Yale University
Designed and built an end-to-end data-ingestion pipeline to transfer assembled and FASTQ-format AIRR-seq data to Yale HPC via igserver; executed nf-core/airrflow with Nextflow/DSL2 and Slurm for V(D)J assignment, novel allele inference, and genotype/haplotype calling. 05/2025 – 01/2026
DrugPlayGround: Benchmarking Large Language Models and Embeddings for Drug Discovery
Research Assistant · Prof. Hongyu Zhao, Yale University
Investigated LLM-based drug representations as an alternative to structure-based molecular embeddings. Benchmarked 5 LLMs and 5 embedding models across drug synergy prediction, drug–protein interaction, and chemical perturbation tasks; curated 4 datasets (1,000+ cancer drug entries each) from PubChem and HuggingFace. Engineered Normal, CoT, and Meta prompting strategies; developed batch-inference pipelines on Yale HPC generating 100k+ drug descriptions. Demonstrated that LLM-derived embeddings outperform state-of-the-art molecular foundation models — up to 54% improvement in drug synergy prediction (R²) and 22% in drug–protein interaction accuracy.
06/2023 – 05/2025
Diabetes Prevention Chatbots to Help Dietary Self-Management for the Chinese Population
Research Investigator · Prof. Ming-Chun Huang, Duke Kunshan University
Investigated the use of LLMs for digital health interventions in culturally tailored diabetes prevention. Curated a China-specific guideline dataset from 5+ academic papers; fine-tuned GPT-3.5-turbo and GPT-4o via OpenAI API with scenario-based evaluation combining quantitative metrics and qualitative assessment. Developed and deployed an AI-driven Streamlit web app with Firebase authentication and real-time chat history export. Published first-author paper at IEEE EMBC 2025.
