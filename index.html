LLM-based Clinical AI Agents for EHR Phenotyping
Research Assistant · Prof. Hua Xu, Yale University
Implemented eMERGE algorithms for T2DM, Dementia, and Hypothyroidism using DuckDB and SQL on YNHH OMOP data. Achieved unlabeled-inclusive PPV / F1 of 74.5% / 80.0% (T2DM), 73.2% / 75.3% (Dementia), 15.1% / 26.0% (Hypothyroidism).

Research Assistant · Prof. Hongyu Zhao, Yale University
