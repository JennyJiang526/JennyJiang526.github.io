I am a master's student in Biostatistics at Yale University, advised by Prof. Hongyu Zhao. My research focuses on large language models for drug discovery, LLM-powered digital health interventions, and AI applications in precision medicine.

I am broadly interested in developing and evaluating AI systems — from benchmarking LLMs and embeddings for biomedical tasks to building culturally tailored chatbots for disease prevention in underserved populations.
