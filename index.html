Synthetic Cohort Generation and Robustness Studies for AI-based Clinical Trial Matching
Research Assistant · Prof. Tina Hernandez-Boussard, Stanford University · Mentor: Soumyadeep Roy

LLM-based Clinical AI Agents for EHR Phenotyping
Research Assistant · Prof. Hua Xu, Yale University

Achieved unlabeled-inclusive PPV / F1 of 74.5% / 80.0% (T2DM), 73.2% / 75.3% (Dementia), 15.1% / 26.0% (Hypothyroidism).
