Who I Am

A Few Things About Me

🌏
Between Two Worlds
I grew up in China and spent my college years moving between DKU in Kunshan and Duke in Durham — now at Yale in New Haven. Living across two cultures has made me deeply aware of how context shapes meaning, and that perspective quietly informs everything I build: AI systems that should work for people, not just on paper.
📷
Seeing Through a Lens
Photography taught me to look before I interpret. Whether I'm framing a street scene on a trip abroad or thinking about how to visualize model outputs, the instinct is the same — find the angle that tells the most honest story. I'm drawn to light, texture, and the quiet moments between the obvious ones.
✈️
Always in Motion
Travel is where I recharge and recalibrate. New places disrupt my assumptions — which I've come to think of as a feature. Some of my clearest thinking has happened somewhere completely unfamiliar, usually with bad Wi-Fi and good food. I'm always planning the next trip.
